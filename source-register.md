## Verified project statements

1. The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is the Government of India's online portal for cybercrime complaints. [S1–S3]
2. The 1930 helpline is designated for assistance with financial cyber-fraud reporting. If money has been lost, report immediately and contact the bank/payment provider through its official channel. [S4]
3. The portal accepts different categories of cybercrime complaints; users should follow the current portal instructions and retain the acknowledgement details. [S1–S2]
4. The portal routes complaints to law-enforcement agencies. It does not itself guarantee recovery or determine the freezing/unfreezing of bank accounts. [S3, S7]
5. The IT Act contains provisions including sections 66, 66C, 66D, 66E, 67, 67A and 67B. Their application depends on the facts and current law. [S5–S6]

## Claims deliberately not made

- No promise that a victim will recover money.
- No claim that every complaint is resolved within a fixed number of hours or days.
- No universal 24-hour portal completion deadline: instructions may be specific to a particular workflow or document.
- No claim that the portal itself freezes or unfreezes bank accounts.
- No legal conclusion that a particular section definitely applies to an individual incident.
- No use of IT Act Section 66A as a current offence provision. Verify omitted / struck-down provisions against the latest official text.
