# Five Cyber Safety Awareness Posts

## Post 1 — UPI / payment fraud
**Pause before you pay.** Never share your OTP or UPI PIN with anyone. If you lose money in a cyber financial fraud, call **1930 immediately**, contact your bank through its official channel and report at https://cybercrime.gov.in/. Keep your transaction reference and complaint acknowledgement.

## Post 2 — Fake customer care
**A search result is not proof.** Fraudsters may publish fake customer-care numbers. Use the phone number inside your bank's official app, website or statement. Never install a remote-access app because an unknown caller asks you to.

## Post 3 — Account security
**Your account. Your access.** Use a unique password, enable multi-factor authentication and review active sessions. If your account is hacked, use the platform's official recovery route and warn your contacts about suspicious messages.

## Post 4 — Impersonation
**A familiar profile can still be fake.** Verify unexpected money requests through another trusted channel. Save the profile URL and report impersonation to the platform and the official cybercrime portal.

## Post 5 — Evidence and support
**Don't delete the evidence. Don't face it alone.** Preserve messages, URLs, transaction references and timestamps. If you are threatened or in immediate danger, contact local police/emergency services. Report cybercrime through https://cybercrime.gov.in/.

*Campaign note: Adapt the wording to the platform and audience. Do not use real victim identities, private screenshots or unverified statistics.*
