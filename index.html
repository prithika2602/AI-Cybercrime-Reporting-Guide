FIRST RESPONSE

What to do first

Keep calm. Do not delete messages, emails, transaction alerts or other original evidence.

01
◉

Stop further loss

For financial fraud, contact 1930 immediately. Contact your bank or payment provider using its official app, website or card statement contact details.

02
▣

Secure your accounts

From a trusted device, change compromised passwords, sign out unknown sessions and enable multi-factor authentication. Never share an OTP or PIN.

03
⌁

Preserve evidence

Save screenshots, URLs, phone numbers, email headers, chat records, transaction IDs, dates and acknowledgement numbers. Keep originals unchanged.

04
↗

Report officially

Use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. For urgent danger or immediate threats, contact local police/emergency services as appropriate.

!

Important: Reporting does not guarantee recovery of money or immediate account blocking. The portal routes complaints to law-enforcement agencies; follow up with your bank and the relevant police authority.

LEGAL AWARENESS

Cyber law, in simple terms

These are examples of provisions in Indian law that may be relevant. The exact provision depends on facts and legal assessment.

IT ACT · 2000

Section 66

Computer-related offences involving dishonest or fraudulent acts covered by the Act.

IT ACT · 2000

Section 66C

Identity theft: fraudulent or dishonest use of another person’s electronic signature, password or unique identification feature.

IT ACT · 2000

Section 66D

Cheating by personation using a communication device or computer resource.

IT ACT · 2000

Section 66E

Violation of privacy through capturing, publishing or transmitting images of a private area in circumstances covered by the section.

IT ACT · 2000

Sections 67 / 67A

Provisions relating to publishing or transmitting obscene material and sexually explicit material in electronic form.

IT ACT · 2000

Section 67B

Provision relating to material depicting children in sexually explicit acts in electronic form. Do not download, save or forward such material.

i

Legal caution: This is a basic awareness summary, not legal advice. Laws, amendments, procedures and interpretations can change. Do not rely on an AI-generated section number or penalty without checking the current official text and consulting a qualified professional.
