# Five “What to do if…” Guides

## 1. If money was taken through UPI / online financial fraud
1. Call **1930 immediately**.
2. Contact your bank, wallet or payment provider through its official app/site/statement channel.
3. File the complaint on [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) and follow the current portal workflow.
4. Keep transaction ID/UTR, amount, date/time, bank or wallet details, SMS alerts and screenshots.
5. Never share OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, passwords or screen-sharing access.
6. Retain the complaint acknowledgement and follow up with the bank and police authority.

## 2. If an email, social media or messaging account was hacked
1. Start recovery only through the official platform.
2. Change the password from a trusted device; change reused passwords elsewhere.
3. End unknown sessions and remove unfamiliar recovery methods or app permissions.
4. Enable MFA and secure the linked email and mobile number.
5. Warn contacts about suspicious messages or payment requests.
6. Preserve login alerts, messages, profile changes and timestamps.
7. Report the incident through the portal; contact local police where appropriate.

## 3. If someone is impersonating you or stealing your identity
1. Preserve the fake profile URL, username, contact details, messages and timestamps.
2. Report the fake account to the platform using its impersonation process.
3. Secure any affected accounts and contact affected financial institutions through official channels.
4. File a complaint at the official cybercrime portal and keep the acknowledgement.
5. Tell friends/family not to transfer money or disclose information to the fake account.

## 4. If you face online harassment, stalking or threats
1. If physical harm is imminent, contact local police/emergency services immediately.
2. Do not retaliate or agree to meet the person.
3. Preserve messages, call logs, profile URLs, dates, times and screenshots.
4. Use platform block/report tools when safe; review privacy settings.
5. Tell a trusted person and seek appropriate support.
6. File a portal complaint and/or contact local police as appropriate.

## 5. If private or intimate content is shared or threatened
1. Prioritise immediate safety; contact local police/emergency services if in danger.
2. Preserve safe evidence such as URLs, account handles, message text, timestamps and report IDs.
3. Do not download, save, screenshot, forward or repost intimate material.
4. Report the content to the platform and the official cybercrime portal.
5. If a child may be depicted, do not access, save, copy or share the material; report immediately to the portal/police.
6. Do not pay an extorter or send additional material. Seek trusted and qualified support.
