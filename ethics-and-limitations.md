# Responsible AI, Ethics and Limitations

## Defensive and educational purpose
This project is designed for public awareness, safe reporting guidance and legal literacy. It must not be used to create malware, phishing pages, credential theft forms, exploit instructions or deceptive training links.

## Privacy
- Do not collect or store real victim data.
- Do not enter passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, CVVs, full card numbers or identity-document numbers.
- Do not upload private chats, screenshots, company data or confidential records to AI tools.
- The checklist builder operates locally in the browser and does not transmit the selected situation or checked items to a server.
- Do not download, save or forward intimate content or child sexual abuse material.

## AI limitations
AI systems can hallucinate or repeat outdated information. Common high-risk errors include:
- Outdated or invented helpline numbers.
- Fake government URLs.
- Incorrect portal workflows or fixed deadlines.
- Invented legal provisions, penalties or claims that a law definitely applies.
- Unsafe advice to delete messages, confront a suspect or pay a recovery agent.
- False promises that money will be recovered.

## Human verification
Every important claim must be checked against official government portals and current legislation. Human reviewers must make final decisions. AI is not a police authority, bank, emergency service or legal adviser.

## Accessibility and inclusion
Use plain language, responsive layouts, clear labels and keyboard-operable controls. Avoid blaming victims. Do not assume a person has access to a laptop, bank branch or technical knowledge.

## Testing boundaries
Use fictional sample cases. Do not test on real victims, accounts, websites, networks or services. Any security testing outside a self-owned isolated lab requires written authorisation.
