# Research Notes and AI-Assisted Method

## Research questions
1. Which common cybercrime situations should an Indian public-awareness guide explain?
2. Which official channels should a person use to report cybercrime and financial cyber fraud?
3. Which basic IT Act provisions are useful to introduce without giving legal advice?

## Method
- Use at least two AI research tools to identify relevant topics and draft summaries.
- Compare the answers with official Government of India sources listed in `source-register.md`.
- Record the exact prompt, tool name, date, answer excerpt, source URL and verification status.
- Use at least four AI tools for the capstone comparison if required by the instructor; do not claim a tool was used unless you actually tested it.
- Keep a human reviewer responsible for all final reporting and legal content.

## Findings supported by official sources
- The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is the official online route for cybercrime complaints.
- The 1930 helpline is used for assistance with financial cyber-fraud reporting.
- The portal has categories covering multiple cybercrime types; users must follow current portal instructions.
- Selected IT Act provisions include sections 66, 66C, 66D, 66E, 67, 67A and 67B.

## Open questions for student verification
- Do the portal screens or categories change after this review date?
- Are there state-specific police reporting or support resources relevant to the selected demonstration?
- Have any statutory amendments changed the wording or interpretation of the listed provisions?
- Did the AI tools produce any incorrect helpline, deadline, section or penalty?

## Research integrity
Do not invent statistics, tool test results, peer feedback or time savings. Use the CSV templates to record actual results.
